Your questions

Frequently asked questions.

Straight answers on the products, shipping and returns.

Which jaw trainer level should I start with?
Beginner if it is your first trainer. Move up to Advanced when a full session feels easy, and to Expert only after weeks on Advanced.
When will I see results?
The pump shows right after a jaw session; visible definition builds over the first weeks with consistent training. Tapes work from the first night. Patches flatten a spot overnight.
Is mouth tape safe?
Only if you can breathe freely through your nose. Do not use it with a blocked nose, after alcohol, or with a breathing or sleep condition without medical advice. Not for children.
What are the products made of?
The trainer is food-grade silicone, free of BPA, PVC and PFAS. Tapes use a skin-friendly adhesive, single use. Patches are hydrocolloid, single use.
How long does shipping take?
Orders ship from the United States and arrive in 3 to 5 business days. Shipping is free in the US.
Can I return a product?
Yes, within 14 days of delivery, unused and sealed. Tapes and patches must be unopened. Email support@aesthetic-lab.com with your order number.
Is there a warranty?
The JAWESTHETIC® 3.0 trainer has a 1-year free replacement warranty.
What is a protocol?
A 90-day step-by-step system for one pillar of your appearance (face, skin, hair, physique, grooming, style), delivered instantly online. The free assessment tells you which one to start with.
