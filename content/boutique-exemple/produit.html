How it works
  • Prepare. Wash both pieces with warm water. Place one on the molars on each side, over the last 2 to 3 teeth.
  • 2 min, slow. Bite all the way down, hold one second at the limit, release. Controlled reps until the jaw feels tired.
  • 2 min, fast. Switch to quick, controlled chewing without stopping.
  • 2 min break. That is one set. Repeat 3 to 5 sets, every other day.
  • Progress. Too easy? Lengthen the intervals, add reps, or move up a level.

Your questions

Frequently asked questions.

Straight answers on levels, training, safety and delivery.

Which level should I choose?
Start with Beginner if it is your first jaw trainer. Move up to Advanced when a full session feels easy, usually after a few weeks, and to Expert only after weeks on Advanced.
How often should I train?
3 to 5 sets every other day: 2 minutes slow, 2 minutes fast, 2 minutes break. Keep intensive sessions between 10 and 20 minutes and give the muscles a rest day in between.
When will I see results?
The pump shows right after a session. With consistent training, definition builds from the first weeks, and people around you notice before you do.
Is it safe?
Food-grade silicone, chewed on the molars with minimal jaw movement. Not for under 14. Stop if you feel pain, and ask your doctor or orthodontist first if you have jaw problems.
What does the warranty cover?
1 year free replacement. If the trainer fails under normal use, we send a new one.
Where does it ship from?
From the United States. Delivery times and costs are shown at checkout.
